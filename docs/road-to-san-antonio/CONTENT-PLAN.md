## Phases

### Phase 1 — LAUNCH (late Aug – early Sep)
- Announcement post + Reel: Malachias to camera, "we got invited" (Episode 01 on the site).
- Explain the goal ($12,000) and the three ways to help; pin the post.
- Story sequence: the invitation → the map (Coral Springs → San Antonio) → the Cash App card → link sticker.
- First-supporters thank-you (names only with permission).

### Phase 2 — BUILD MOMENTUM (September)
- Episode 02 "Why Veterans Day Matters" — founder to camera, 60–90 s.
- Episode 03 "Meet the Band" — one Reel per member (JC, Efraín, Gabe, Malachias), reuse the site bios.
- Episode 04 "Rehearsal" — 15–30 s rehearsal clips, songs from the set.
- Sponsor outreach begins (docs/OUTREACH.md); "Meet our first sponsor" as soon as one confirms.
- Merch reveal when the Road to San Antonio collection is live on Fourthwall.
- Weekly progress Story: the site's progress bar screenshot + remaining amount.

### Phase 3 — ACCELERATE (October)
- Episode 05 "25% Funded" / Episode 06 "Meet Our Sponsors" / Episode 07 "Halfway There" — post the milestone the day the admin number crosses it.
- More rehearsal footage, setlist teasers, behind the scenes.
- Community push: churches, veteran groups, local businesses in South Florida and San Antonio.
- Urgency: "X days · $Y to go" Story every 3–4 days.

### Phase 4 — FINAL PUSH (Nov 1–11)
- Daily countdown Story with the remaining amount.
- Episode 08 "Preparing for Texas" (packing, cases, flights) and Episode 09 "Final Rehearsal".
- Last sponsor call, last merch push (order-by date for delivery before the trip).
- Thank-you wall: every sponsor tagged.

### Phase 5 — EVENT (Nov 12)
- Travel day content (airport, San Antonio arrival), backstage, soundcheck, the performance (clips within the organizer's rules), Veterans Day message from the founder.

### Phase 6 — AFTER
- Episode 10 "We Made It": photos/video, thank supporters and sponsors by name, publish the campaign result (raised vs goal, what it paid for).
- Set the admin status to `completed` — the page becomes the record; sponsors remain recognized.
