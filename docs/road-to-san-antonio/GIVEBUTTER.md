## Why Givebutter (verified against givebutter.com/pricing, Aug 2026)
- 0 % platform fee when donor tips are enabled (3 % flat if disabled); card processing 2.9 % + 30¢ / ACH 1.9 % + 30¢ (covered by donor tips when enabled; Givebutter says 92 % of donors cover fees).
- Payouts to a connected bank in 2–3 business days; verified nonprofits: 48-hour pending period, instant payouts eligible later.
- Campaign types: Form, **Page**, Event, Auction. **Peer-to-peer works on Page and Event campaigns only** — we use a **Page** campaign.
- One platform, one goal, one donor list. No GoFundMe/Kickstarter in parallel.

## Accounting — one number, every dollar sourced
Admin → *Verified support ledger*: one row per reconciliation with **source** (Givebutter donations · Cash App donations · Merch net contribution · Cash sponsorships), amount as received, date, and a reference (payout ID, activity window, sponsor). The ledger total **becomes** the public "raised" number; in-kind value is tracked separately (*In-kind support*) and shown as "Plus $X in confirmed in-kind support". Numbers are never merged until they are reconciled rows.
