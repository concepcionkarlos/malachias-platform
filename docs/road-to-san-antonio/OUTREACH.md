Copy, adapt, send by hand. Nothing here is sent automatically. Replace the bracketed parts.

## Outreach segments

**Miami / South Florida** — veteran-owned businesses (Broward/Miami-Dade veteran business directories, chambers), churches and Christian communities the band has played or attended, the local music community (studios, rehearsal rooms, guitar shops), local businesses near Coral Springs, musicians, friends and family networks, military families and VFW/American Legion posts.

**San Antonio / Texas** — veteran organizations and veteran-owned businesses in Bexar County, churches with military ministries, rock venues and communities, music stores, local restaurants and hotels near the event, military communities (JBSA), local media (radio, veteran podcasts, community papers).

**National** — veteran organizations, Christian rock/metal communities and playlists, veteran Facebook groups (post as a member, follow group rules — no spamming), military-support businesses, guitar and music companies with artist/community programs.

Approach: personal messages one at a time, 10–15 per day, tracked in the admin (Sponsor inquiries) or a simple sheet. No scraping, no bulk email.
