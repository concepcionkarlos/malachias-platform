# Road to San Antonio — on-camera scripts (for Malachias) + captions

Written to be **said**, not read: short lines, one breath each, plain words. Malachias speaks English on camera; notes in *italics* are direction for Juan (in Spanish). Keep every clip under 60 s for Reels; the longer versions are for Facebook/YouTube.

Rules that apply to every script: no "tax deductible", no promises about the event's stage or signage, no numbers we haven't confirmed. Always end with the same three words on screen: **ROAD TO SAN ANTONIO**.

Recipient line to show on screen whenever money is mentioned: **Warfighter Gardens · $AWarriorsGarden**

---

## 01 · WE GOT INVITED (announcement · 40–50 s)

*Plano medio, sentado o de pie con la guitarra, luz natural. Mira a cámara. Ritmo tranquilo; la primera frase con una pausa después.*

> We got the invitation.
>
> Malachias has been asked to play a Veterans Day event in San Antonio, Texas — November twelfth.
>
> I served two tours in Iraq. This band exists for the people who came home carrying what nobody talks about. A Veterans Day stage is the reason we started.
>
> Now we have to get the whole band there. Flights, instruments, a place to sleep. We're doing it the way we do everything — with the people who believe in this.
>
> The campaign is live at malachiasmusic dot com. It's called the Road to San Antonio.
>
> Walk it with us.

**Caption:** WE'RE GOING TO SAN ANTONIO. Malachias has been invited to perform for Veterans Day 2026. Now we're working to bring the full band. November 12 · San Antonio, TX. Support through Cash App → Warfighter Gardens · $AWarriorsGarden. Campaign → link in bio. #RoadToSanAntonio #VeteransDay2026 #MalachiasBand

## 02 · WHY VETERANS DAY MATTERS (founder · 60–90 s)

*Más íntimo: cerca de cámara, sin música al principio. Que hable despacio. Corte a fotos del Ejército si las tiene (reales).*

> Veterans Day isn't a holiday for me. It's a roll call.
>
> I came home from Iraq and I didn't know who I was anymore. Music cracked me open again. Faith came through the crack.
>
> Every song we play is for the guy sitting in his truck in a church parking lot who can't go inside yet. For the family that hasn't heard their son talk in four years.
>
> On November twelfth we get to play for a room full of people who understand that without a single word.
>
> That's why we're going. That's why we're asking for help to get the whole band there.
>
> Road to San Antonio. Link's below.

## Filming checklist (para Juan)

- Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Teléfono a la altura de los ojos, a 1 m.
- Luz de frente (ventana), nunca detrás. Mic del teléfono vale si la sala está en silencio; si hay ensayo, un lavalier.
- Cada guion: 3 tomas. Que diga las frases cortas de una en una; se cortan después.
- Deja 2 s de silencio al principio y al final de cada toma (para los subtítulos y el logo).
- Subtítulos siempre (la mayoría ve sin sonido). Palabras clave en dorado: SAN ANTONIO, VETERANS DAY, ROAD.
- Cierra siempre con la tarjeta: emblema · ROAD TO SAN ANTONIO · Nov 12 · malachiasmusic.com.
