# The Malachias message — four cuts, EN + ES

One thing Malachias recorded on his phone (95 s, vertical, talking straight to camera)
becomes four posts. Source and generator live in `videos/malachias-message/`; the
rendered files land in `~/Desktop/RTSA-videos-v2/`.

| Cut | Length | What it is | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `free` | 24 s | "We're offering to play for free — donations are only for the travel." | The trust post. Best single answer to *"why are you asking for money?"* |
| `band` | 25 s | "Either I go alone with an acoustic, or the whole band goes." | The unlock. This is the one that makes people give — it turns a donation into a decision. |
| `money` | 22 s | "We don't make money on this. Everything goes to getting there, staying, and getting home." | Transparency. Pair with the budget section on the campaign page. |
| `message` | 97 s | The full message, top to bottom, including how to donate. | The website, the pinned Facebook post, the sponsor email. Too long for Reels. |

Post order, if you only run one a week: **band → free → money**. Lead with the
decision, back it with the trust, close with the receipts.

## The layout rules — do not break these

His framing is a low selfie: measured on a 5 % grid, **his hair starts at 40–45 %
of frame height and his beard bottoms out at 75–78 %**, with ~40 % of empty
screened patio above his head. That geometry dictates everything:

```
   0 %  ── empty sky ................. chip + date, and the one HERO phrase
  38 %  ──────────────────────────────  top of his hair
         HIS FACE — nothing is ever drawn here
  78 %  ──────────────────────────────  bottom of his beard
  80 %  ── captions (the "rail"), over his dark shirt
  93 %  ── the call to action
```

- **Captions live in the bottom band only.** The first version of this edit
  anchored them by padding, which put them at ~63 % of frame height — directly on
  his mouth. They are now pinned to a fixed band that cannot drift.
- **One promoted phrase per cut, not more.** The big gold line in the sky
  (`FOR FREE`, `THE ENTIRE BAND`, `TO GET US THERE`) lands on the exact beat he
  says it. Two of them in one cut and neither one lands.
- **Never two captions on screen at once.** They share one box; each hands off
  0.04 s before the next enters.
- **Break captions on clauses, not on word count.** A caption ending in *"and"*
  or *"because it is"* is the amateur tell.
- **The footage is never graded, cropped or recoloured.** Captions are the only
  thing added.

## The "how to donate" section — now matched by the site

The stretch from 62 s to 76 s ("I'm going to put a link in the comments… you can
donate through Cash App, you can donate through PayPal, you can contact me
directly and I can send you a tax deductible thing") **is included in the
`message` cut**, and as of 2026-09-01 the site backs all of it:

- **PayPal** is live on the campaign page next to Cash App —
  `Warfighterup@gmail.com`, in `CAMPAIGN.paypal`.
- **Tax deductibility** is stated on the campaign page. `nonprofitVerified` is
  now `true`, and `CAMPAIGN.nonprofit` carries the legal name and EIN.

The EIN was checked against the IRS record before any of that wording shipped:

| | |
|---|---|
| Legal name | Warfighter Gardens |
| EIN | 81-4794313 |
| Location | Coral Springs, FL |
| Subsection | 3 → 501(c)(3) |
| Ruling year | 2017 |
| Deductibility code | 1 → contributions are deductible |

The published wording is *"Contributions are tax deductible to the extent allowed
by law"* plus a receipt contact — the standard form. Do not tighten it to a flat
"your donation is tax deductible": what any individual can actually deduct depends
on their own return, and the softer phrasing is both accurate and what the IRS
record supports.

## Rebuilding

```bash
cd videos/malachias-message
python3 build.py <free|band|money|message> <en|es>   # writes index.html
npx hyperframes check                                # 0 errors before rendering
npx hyperframes snapshot --at 2,8,14 -o snaps        # look at it before paying for a render
npx hyperframes render -o out.mp4
```

Everything — the cuts, the Spanish rail, the hero phrases, the safe geometry — is
in `build.py`. The generated `index.html` is disposable; edit the Python, never
the HTML.

**Still open:** the source is a WhatsApp-compressed 478×850 copy, upscaled 2.3× to
1080×1920. If Malachias sends the original file off his phone, re-running the same
commands gives noticeably sharper video with no other changes.
