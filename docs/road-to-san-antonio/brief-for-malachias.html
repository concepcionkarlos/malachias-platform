Road to San Antonio · Veterans Day 2026 · for Malachias

What to film, what to say, when to send it

You record on your phone and send the raw clips to JC. He edits, adds captions in English and Spanish, and posts everything. You never have to edit or post anything yourself.

The one rule: short lines, one breath each, then pause. Say each sentence separately — JC cuts between them. Three takes of every clip. Don't worry about mistakes: keep rolling and say the line again.

How to film (5 things)

VerticalPhone upright, 9:16. Eye level, about 1 m / 3 ft away.
Light in frontFace a window or lamp. Never a window behind you.
Quiet roomPhone mic is fine if the room is silent. No music playing.
2 seconds of silenceBefore you start and after you finish each take.
Look at the lensNot at the screen. Guitar on or nearby — it's your uniform now.
Send originalsAirDrop or a shared iCloud/Google Drive folder. Not WhatsApp (it crushes quality).

How to send

  • One folder per batch: RTSA-Batch1, RTSA-Batch2…
  • Original files, no compression (AirDrop, iCloud link or Google Drive).
  • Name the files with the clip number: 01-invited-take2.mov.
  • Text JC when the folder is up. He edits and posts; you approve the first one only.

What else moves the road (besides videos)

  • The organizer. Forward the invitation email to JC and ask them what they cover: airfare, hotel, meals, ground transport, backline, PA, baggage, passes, artist fee. Every "yes" lowers the goal.
  • Facebook Page. The site links to your personal profile. Create a Page "Malachias" (or make JC an admin of one if it exists). Without it there are no ads, no insights and no Instagram link.
  • Five names. Write down five business owners, pastors or veteran contacts who'd take your call. JC sends the sponsor email; you follow up with a call.
  • Photos we don't have. Your Army photos (real ones), the flag guitar close-up, the shirt on you in daylight, any live-show photo of the four current members.
  • Voice notes. 20-second voice memos from the truck ("Day 12 of the road…") become Stories with captions — no camera needed.
  • Share on your own profile the day each video goes up, with one personal sentence. Your friends list is the first audience.
  • Cash App check. Once a week, open Cash App activity and text JC the total so far. That number goes on the site.
  • Set a rehearsal date where all four record their 15-second intros and the b-roll in one hour.
